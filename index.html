Privacy Policy

CandyMandy

A match-3 puzzle game by Rohit Nishad. This page explains what information the app touches, where it goes, and how to reach us about it.

Effective October 19, 2026 App ID com.example.candymandy Contact rohitnishad737@gmail.com

01 Overview

CandyMandy is a single-player puzzle game. It does not require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email address, or any other personal detail to play.

The app shows advertising through Google AdMob to support free play. That is the only outside party CandyMandy shares any device information with, and the sections below describe exactly what that involves.

02 Information we collect directly

CandyMandy itself does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on a server. The only data the app keeps is your in-game progress — level reached, score, and settings — saved locally on your device using Android's standard preferences storage.

This local save data:

  • Never leaves your device or gets sent to us or anyone else
  • Is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage
  • Is not linked to your name, email, or any account

03 Advertising Google AdMob

CandyMandy displays banner and interstitial ads using the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). To select and measure ads, Google and its advertising partners may automatically collect technical information from your device, independently of anything CandyMandy itself collects.

Data typePurpose
Advertising IDAd selection, frequency capping, measurement
IP address / approximate locationAd delivery, fraud prevention
Device & app info (model, OS, app version)Ad compatibility, performance reporting
Ad interaction (views, clicks)Measurement and billing

This processing is governed by Google's own policies, not CandyMandy's. You can review how Google handles this data and the controls available to you at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

To limit ad personalization, use your device's ad settings: on Android, go to Settings › Privacy › Ads (or Google Settings › Ads on older versions) and opt out of interest-based ads, or reset your advertising ID.

04 App permissions

CandyMandy requests only the permissions it needs to function:

PermissionWhy it's needed
Internet accessLoading ads from Google AdMob
Network stateChecking connectivity before requesting an ad
VibrationHaptic feedback when candies match or swap

CandyMandy does not request access to your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, storage, or any other sensitive permission.

05 Children's privacy

CandyMandy is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us using the details below and we will address it.

06 Data security

Because CandyMandy keeps game data on your device only, there is no server-side database of your personal information for us to protect. Data handled by the AdMob SDK is secured according to Google's own infrastructure and policies.

07 Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example, if a future update adds a new feature that collects data — we'll revise this page and update the effective date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the revised policy.
